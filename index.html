How it works

How Synapse Bridge moves a swap across chains

Synapse Bridge keeps the flow direct: your wallet starts the transfer, the app shows the live route, and your confirmation sends the transaction on-chain.

1

Connect your wallet

Open the app with a self-custody wallet. You stay in control of approvals and signatures from the first step.

2

Choose route and asset

Select the source chain, destination chain and supported asset. Use ETH, USDC or another listed token when that route is available in the app.

3

Review and confirm

Check the quoted output, timing, route and network cost, then sign only if the transaction matches what you want to do.

FAQ

Synapse Bridge questions

01What is Synapse Bridge?

Synapse Bridge is a non-custodial DeFi bridge and swap app for moving supported assets across chains. You connect a wallet, choose a route and sign the transfer yourself.

02Is Synapse Bridge non-custodial?

Yes. You keep the private keys, approvals and transaction signatures in your wallet. The app gives you a bridge route, but it does not create a custodial account for your funds.

03Which chains and assets can I bridge?

Available routes depend on live app support. Ethereum, L2 ecosystems and EVM networks are typical bridge destinations; see the supported chains page before planning a transfer.

04How long does a bridge transfer take?

Time depends on the selected networks, confirmation conditions and route. Synapse Bridge shows the current estimate in the app before you sign.

05What does Synapse Bridge cost?

The final cost depends on the route and on-chain fees such as Ethereum gas. Review the live quote and wallet prompt before confirming.

06How should I think about safety?

Use the app from the correct URL, inspect the wallet request, avoid blind approvals and understand bridge risk. Independent dashboards such as L2Beat network research can help you compare chain assumptions.
