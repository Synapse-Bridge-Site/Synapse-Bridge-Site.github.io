What is Synapse Bridge?

Synapse is an interoperability protocol and asset-transfer app for routing tokens between supported chains; the official docs describe support for 20+ EVM and non-EVM blockchains. Use the app only after checking the domain and wallet prompt.

How Synapse Bridge works

  1. Open the appGo to bridge.synapseprotocol.com and connect your wallet only after verifying the URL.
  2. Choose the routeSelect the origin chain, destination chain, token, and amount you want to send.
  3. Review the quoteCheck the output token, route details, slippage, and required network before signing.
  4. Confirm and trackApprove the token only if needed, sign in your wallet, then track status in the app or explorer.

Before you bridge

Synapse Router can return bridge functions based on the selected chains and tokens, including canonical, liquidity pool, and CCTP paths.

Broad network coverage

The product is built for EVM and non-EVM ecosystems, with route availability shown in the official app and Synapse documentation.

Synapse Bridge FAQ

What is synapse bridge?

synapse bridge is the Synapse app flow for moving supported crypto assets between chains using route quotes and wallet-confirmed transactions.

Is synapse bridge the official app?

The official action URL is bridge.synapseprotocol.com; this page is a routing and education page, not the official app.

Which networks does synapse bridge support?

Synapse docs reference 20+ EVM and non-EVM blockchains, but available routes depend on the token, chain pair, and current app support.

How do I use synapse bridge safely?

Verify the domain, review the route and amount, check wallet approval prompts, and confirm you are on the intended origin and destination networks.

Why did my synapse bridge quote change?

Quotes can change with route availability, liquidity, token amount, and network conditions; refresh the quote and rely on the current wallet prompt before signing.
